Jipange Account

Your goals matter. The Jipange Account helps you save steadily toward the things you care about most.

A Savings Plan with Purpose

Jipange helps you set a clear savings goal and build discipline as you work toward it.

What you get

Goal-oriented savings to help you stay on track

Earn interest

Up to 3 free withdrawals per year

Deposit any amount, any time 

Easy top up via MPESA using Paybill 544700

Access to financing based on your savings and ability to repay

What you need

Be a registered Unaitas member

Hold at least KES 1,000 in share capital

Initial deposit to activate the account

Defined savings goal or purpose 

Simple to Join. Easy to Use.

Opening a Timiza Transactional Account takes just a few clear steps .

Step 1

Become a Member

Open a Unaitas membership account and join a community built to help members grow financially.

Step 2

Open Your jipange Account

Choose the Jipange Account and set the savings goal you want to work toward.

Step 3

Start Saving

Deposit regularly through Unaitas channels or via M-PESA Paybill 544700 and watch your savings grow.
