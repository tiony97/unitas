Delegates

Delegates are democratically elected by members from specific zones to represent their interests within the Sacco. They serve as a vital link between members and Unaitas leadership, ensuring that communication, accountability and transparency are upheld at all levels. Delegates also play a key role in governance by electing the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Committee.

Key Roles of Our Delegates:

Present member views, feedback and concerns at the Annual General Meetings (AGMs) and Special General Meetings (SGMs).

Facilitate communication between Unaitas and members by sharing updates, AGM deliberations and Sacco developments.

Guide members in understanding Unaitas products and services to help them make informed financial decisions.

Actively participate in discussions, voting and decision-making processes at AGMs

Exercise independent judgment and authority to protect and promote the interests of all members.
