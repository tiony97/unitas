Our Legacy

A Legacy of Transformation

Unaitas was established in 1993 by a visionary group of tea farmers who believed in creating financial solutions that serve people with shared goals and ambitions. Since then, we have grown into one of Africa’s largest Saccos, serving individuals, businesses, groups and institutions across Kenya.

We support members from all walks of life, whether you are saving, investing, running a business or managing an institution. Our focus is to provide the financial tools and trusted guidance you need to grow, achieve stability and pursue long-term success.

At Unaitas, we are bringing dreams to life, one member, one solution, one step at a time.

Murang'a Tea Growers SACCO is born
A visionary group of tea farmers in Murang'a pooled their resources to form a Savings and Credit Cooperative Society creating a financial basket where members could save and access affordable loans. Rooted in community. Built on trust.
Branch Network Expansion Begins
The SACCO opens its first branch outside Murang'a town, bringing financial services closer to tea farmers in remote areas. Membership grows as more farmers join the cooperative movement, strengthening the community's economic foundation.
Digital Transformation Initiative
Introduction of mobile banking services revolutionizes how members access their accounts. The SACCO becomes one of the first in the region to offer SMS banking, making transactions faster and more convenient for farmers in rural areas.
Rebranding to Unaitas
A historic rebranding marks the transformation from a traditional SACCO to a modern financial solutions provider. The new identity, Unaitas, reflects the cooperative's expanded vision and commitment to serving diverse member needs across Kenya.

What Makes Us Different

Creating value That Lasts
+ −

As Unaitas, we have developed a strong value proposition for our stakeholders in a sustainable manner. As an organization, we are conscious of the business environment we operate in and recognize the diversity of the society that we serve.

Products Built Around Our Members
+ −

We design financial products that adapt to your unique needs, not the other way around. From flexible savings accounts to customized loan solutions, every product is crafted with our members' goals in mind. Our member-centric approach ensures you get solutions that work for your specific situation, whether you're an individual, business, or group.

Responsible Lending
+ −

We believe in lending that empowers rather than overwhelms. Our responsible lending practices ensure that members receive loans they can comfortably repay, with transparent terms and fair interest rates. We take time to understand your financial situation and provide guidance to help you make informed borrowing decisions that support long-term financial health.

Technology Driving Financial Access
+ −

We leverage cutting-edge technology to make financial services accessible anytime, anywhere. Our mobile banking app, USSD codes, and online platforms ensure you can manage your accounts, apply for loans, and access services even in remote areas. Technology breaks down barriers and brings financial empowerment to your fingertips.
