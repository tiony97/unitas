Secure Savings That
Work for You

Looking to grow your money with guaranteed returns? The Unaitas Fixed Deposit Account helps you earn more by locking in your funds for a fixed period, with attractive interest rates and flexible options tailored to your needs.

What you get

Start saving with as little as KES 5,000

No maximum limit on deposits

Choose flexible investment terms of 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months

Earn interest monthly or at maturity

What you need

Open to all registered Unaitas members 

Must hold at least KES 1000 in share capital

Must have an active savings account

Simple to Apply. Easy to Access.

Follow a few simple steps and receive support for your goals.

Step 1

Confirm Your Membership

Ensure you are a registered Unaitas member with share capital and an active savings account.

Step 2

Choose Your Deposit Term

Select how long you would like to lock your funds (1–12 months) and the amount you want to invest.

Step 3

Transfer Your Funds

Move funds from your Unaitas savings account to your Fixed Deposit and start earning interest.
