Investment Shares

Owning Unaitas shares makes you a member and part-owner.

Minimum of 100 shares at KES.10 each (KES.1,000)

Shares are non-refundable and not used as collateral

Eligible for dividends from annual surplus

Maximum of 2,000,000 shares (KES. 20,000,000)

Accounts Opened
Upon Joining

Once your membership is approved, the following accounts will be created for you:

Transactional Account

Sacco Deposit Account (Non-withdrawable savings; used as collateral for loan)

Share Capital Account
